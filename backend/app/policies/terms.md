Version 2026-11-18

charge, without any guarantee of availability, accuracy or data retention.

1. **Who may use it.** You must be at least 19 years old (Korean legal drinking age) to create an account.
2. **Your account.** Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens in your account.
3. **Your content.** The bottles, cellars and notes you enter stay yours. We use them only to run the service
   for you (see the Privacy Policy).
4. **AI answers.** Sommelier answers, recommendations, prices and label readings are generated automatically
   and can be wrong. They are not professional advice. Prices are estimates, not offers.
5. **Responsible drinking.** OmniCave does not sell alcohol. Drink responsibly and follow the law.
6. **Ending your account.** You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Privacy. We may close the
   service when the project ends; we will say so in the app beforehand where possible.
7. **Changes.** If these terms change, the app shows the new version and asks you to accept it.

Contact: the OmniCave project team, Hanyang University.
