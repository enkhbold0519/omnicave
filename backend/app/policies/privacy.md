# OmniCave Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-18

This policy explains which personal information OmniCave collects, why, and your rights under Korea's
Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

## 1. What we collect and why

| Information | Purpose | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Username, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash) | Account, login, email codes | Yes |
| Date of birth | Checking that you are 19 or older | Yes |
| Cellars, bottles, positions, drinking history, taste preferences | Running the cellar service and personalising sommelier answers | Yes |
| Sensor readings and photos you upload for label reading | Climate alerts and adding bottles | Only when you use these features |
| Marketing / news emails | Sending news and product updates | No (optional consent) |

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

## 2. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all of the above is deleted immediately.
Email codes expire after 15 minutes. The consent records are deleted together with the account.

## 3. Processing outside Korea

To run the service we use providers whose servers may be outside Korea (see the separate consent):

| Provider | Country | What it processes |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services (server and database) | Australia (Sydney) until the planned move to Seoul | All account and cellar data |
| Vercel (web display hosting) | United States | Web page delivery; no account data stored |
| RunPod (AI sommelier server, when switched on) | United States / other regions | The wine and question sent for an AI answer |
| Amazon SES (email, once enabled) | Korea (Seoul) | Your email address and the code |

## 4. Your rights

You can view and export your data (Profile → Privacy → Export), correct it (Profile), withdraw the optional
consent at any time (Profile → Privacy), and delete your account (Profile → Privacy → Delete account).

## 5. Security

Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2-SHA256). Login tokens and email codes are stored only as hashes. All traffic uses
HTTPS. Each account can only access its own data.

Privacy officer: the OmniCave project team, Hanyang University.
